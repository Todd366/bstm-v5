# Whole-app audit, 2026-10-28 (v5.6.0)

Method: read live data (read-only), exercised every role through the real middleware in tests, ran the browser test on a phone-size screen, and questioned each rule against real life in Botswana.

## Who can do what (the four kinds of people)
| Who | Gets in with | Can do |
|---|---|---|
| Youth | email + password (own token) | Own profile, apply, work, prove, see own money, download/delete own data |
| Business | email + password (own token) | Post jobs (staff approve), accept applicants, approve work, see invoices, pay |
| Staff: finance / field / support / admin | individual email + password | finance: money. field: jobs, trials, audits, businesses, youth. support: view people, reset codes. admin: all of those + system check |
| **Owner** (bstm366@gmail.com, or the emergency key) | staff login with that email | Everything staff can do, plus: team management, edit any record, delete any record (except money) |
Only the owner email is the owner. Nobody else can be promoted to it from the app. The owner account cannot be turned off.

## Reality check on data (live, read-only)
- 21 people on file: 4 look real (real email addresses), 17 are test or old records (names with "Test", example.com emails, or old records with no email/password so nobody can log in).
- 3 businesses (1 real-looking: "Bstm ecosystem"), 5 jobs (2 real-looking).
- Fix: Staff -> Owner tab lists every record, flags suspected tests with the reason, and lets the owner tick and delete. Nothing is deleted automatically.

## Bugs found and fixed in this release
1. Tab bar overflowed on phones: the last tabs (System, Owner) were unreachable. Now scrolls sideways and keeps the active tab in view.
2. Deleted members still counted in "youth" totals and the Youth list. Now excluded.
3. Admin-level staff could manage the team. Now owner only.
4. Deleting a business would have left its jobs ownerless (database sets owner to empty). The new delete removes its jobs with it, and refuses if any job has money records.
5. Nobody could correct a wrong record without the database. Owner can now edit people, businesses and jobs; every edit and delete is logged with who did it.

## Logic questioned (and verdict)
| Rule | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Money records never deleted | Keep. Voiding is the only way to cancel an invoice. |
| Youth with no email cannot reset password | Real gap for old records; owner can edit the email, or delete. |
| A job can be posted by a business and needs staff approval | Keep. |
| "Budget to agree" jobs (budget 0) produce no invoice | Gap: unpaid work possible. Decision needed from owner: require a budget before work starts. |
| Youth share fixed at 70% | Keep; make it a setting later. |
| Staff offers can reach any youth | Fine; deleted ones are excluded from lists and matching. |
| Phone number is not collected | Gap for villages where WhatsApp/phone is the identity. Roadmap Phase 3. |
